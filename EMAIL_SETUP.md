# Email Setup & Configuration Guide

This guide explains how to change the **Sender Email Address** (the store email address that sends order confirmation emails to customers) and generate a **Gmail App Password**.

---

## 📧 How Email Sending Works

- **Sender (`spring.mail.username`)**: Your store/application email address.
- **App Password (`spring.mail.password`)**: A special 16-character security password generated by Google (NOT your regular Gmail password).
- **Recipient (`event.getEmail()`)**: Automatically set to the customer who placed the order.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Instructions to Set Up Your Email

### Step 1: Generate a Gmail App Password

Google requires an **App Password** for applications sending emails via SMTP.

1. Go to your **Google Account**: [https://myaccount.google.com/](https://myaccount.google.com/)
2. Navigate to **Security** on the left menu.
3. Under *How you sign in to Google*, make sure **2-Step Verification** is turned **ON**.
4. Search for **"App passwords"** in the top search bar, or go directly to:  
   👉 [https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords)
5. Enter an App Name (e.g., `E-Commerce Store`) and click **Create**.
6. Google will generate a **16-character code** (e.g., `abcd efgh ijkl mnop`).
7. **Copy this 16-character code** (ignore spaces or remove them).

---

### Step 2: Update `application.properties`

Open `backend/src/main/resources/application.properties` and update the SMTP section (lines 53–58):

```properties
# =============================================
# Email / SMTP Configuration
# =============================================
spring.mail.host=smtp.gmail.com
spring.mail.port=587
spring.mail.username=YOUR_NEW_EMAIL@gmail.com
spring.mail.password=YOUR_16_DIGIT_APP_PASSWORD
spring.mail.properties.mail.smtp.auth=true
spring.mail.properties.mail.smtp.starttls.enable=true
```

> ⚠️ **Important:**
> - `spring.mail.username` MUST be a full valid email address without `www.` (e.g., `myname@gmail.com`).
> - `spring.mail.password` MUST be the 16-character App Password from Step 1.

---

### Step 3: Restart Backend & Test

1. Stop your backend server if running.
2. Restart it in your terminal:
   ```powershell
   cd "backend"
   mvn spring-boot:run
   ```
3. Place an order on the website.
4. Check the customer's inbox for the order confirmation email!

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting Email Errors

| Error | Cause | Solution |
|-------|-------|----------|
| `535 5.7.8 Authentication Failed` | Wrong username/password or regular password used instead of App Password | Ensure 2-Step Verification is ON and use a 16-character Google App Password. |
| `550 5.1.1 User unknown` | Invalid sender or receiver email format | Remove any `www.` or typos from `spring.mail.username`. |
| `MailSendException: Mail server connection failed` | Port 587 blocked by firewall/antivirus | Ensure `spring.mail.port=587` and internet connection is active. |
